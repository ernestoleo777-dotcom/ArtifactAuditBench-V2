# Scope and limitations / 范围与限制

[Home](../README.md) · [中文主页](../README.zh-CN.md)

- **Controlled synthetic construction.** Tasks use known machine-readable observations, claim templates and programmatic renderers. They do not sample the natural prevalence, diversity or ambiguity of scientific errors.
- **Co-design and access.** RuleAudit exploits supported data, claims and rendering representations; it is not a universal oracle or an evidence-pack-only auditor. Model baselines are not independent benchmark designs.
- **Coupled split shifts.** Style and claim-template changes are not independently manipulated; seen/unseen differences do not identify a causal generalization mechanism.
- **Incomplete model runs.** Preserve actual scored populations, unknowns and failures. Backend comparisons and ablations can have unequal denominators.
- **Null incremental result.** HybridAudit did not improve the main RuleAudit result under the reported gate. More detail, a different backend or a larger synthetic benchmark does not establish new method value.
- **Bounded significance semantics.** Encoded confidence-interval comparisons are deterministic benchmark facts, not universal scientific hypothesis tests.
- **No natural-transfer or necessity claim.** The release does not demonstrate natural-artifact generalization, causal LLM value, LLM necessity, universal deterministic sufficiency, general tool-agent superiority or research-integrity certification.
- **Public truth.** Released evaluator labels support inspection and scoring but cannot guarantee that subsequent users or models are unexposed. Published test labels are not a blind evaluation service.
- **Partial reproduction.** Saved labels reproduce selected metrics, not model execution, provider provenance, latency, billing or every diagnostic table. Passing tests is not independent adoption or scientific validation.

受控合成数据、已知模板与渲染器、方法和基准共同设计、不同计分分母及公开真值，均限制了结果的推广范围。不能把缺失材料、模型措辞差异或工程检查通过当成新科学贡献。本仓库保留原V2成果及负结果，不自动开启SAI、repair或V3路线。
